# Requirement mapping (R1–R9)

The table links each requirement of the article (Section 2.5, Table 6) to the on-chain mechanism that implements it, the off-chain counterpart, the tests that exercise it (`test/requirements.test.js`, `test/regressions.test.js`, `test/edge-cases.test.js`, `test/case-study.test.js`) and the residual risk.

| Req. | Requirement | On-chain mechanism (contract · function) | Off-chain counterpart | Tests | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Attribution of every quantity to a source class and an actor | `Consortium.registerActor` (pseudonymous actor ID, organisation, one operational role); every event stores `actor` and `source` (Sensor, Meter, Invoice, OperatorEntry, Estimate); invoice entries require a document ID (`sourceId`), which is logged with the full envelope and schema version in `ActivityRecorded` | Identity records behind the pseudonymous IDs | R1 (6) | Manual entries remain trust-dependent |
| R2 | Device identity and calibration status | `Consortium.registerDevice` (owner organisation, signing key, accuracy class); `recordCalibration` (certification bodies only, certificate hash, validity); `EnvelopeValidation.validate` verifies an EIP-191 signature bound to chain, contract, scope and envelope, including the material code; meter-only variables; re-sent envelopes rejected | Edge gateway holds the device key; calibration certificates in the evidence store | R2 (10) | Drift between calibrations; compromise of a gateway key |
| R3 | Unit and range validation | `Consortium.defineVariable` (unit, phase, admissible range, meter requirement; methodological authority only); range, phase, completeness and clock-skew checks in `EnvelopeValidation`; yield plausibility in `closeBatch` and again when the oil output of a closed batch is corrected | Edge plausibility rules (flow rates, operating periods) | R3 (8) | Systematic meter bias inside the admissible range |
| R4 | Lot identity across custody and transformation | `registerLot` (only while the farm scope is open); `initiateTransfer` (custodian declaration within tolerance of the harvested mass); `confirmReceipt` (designated mill, signed reading of a calibrated weighbridge, tolerance); `createBatch` (received, unique lot IDs only; atomic consumption; declared mixing rule, mass-proportional shares); `closeBatch` (mandatory meter fields, mass balance, yield) | Physical lot labels (QR/RFID) | R4 (8), regression (1) | Mixing inside the mill is declared, not observed |
| R5 | Emission-factor versioning | `FactorRegistry.propose` (method authority or authorised LCA service) / `approve` (a different method-authority account); validity window; `retire`; results must cite a usable factor set at submission and approval; the certificate reports `FactorSetRetired`; earlier result versions are kept as `Superseded` | Factor-set documents (`offchain/factor-sets/*.json`) whose content hash is registered | R5 (4), case study | Factor quality depends on registry governance |
| R6 | Functional unit, system boundary and allocation rule | `CarbonCertification.submitResult` requires boundary, functional unit, allocation rule, factor set, engine version, report hash, evidence manifest, methodological status, limitation flags and assessment hash; the functional unit must be expressed in the unit of the measured output; phase results must add up to the total | Calculation report (`offchain/lca-engine.js`) | R6 (5), case study | Comparability requires sector conventions |
| R7 | Correction without deletion of history | `proposeCorrection` (same organisation; same governed variable and unit; replacement validated like a new event, pending); `authorizeCorrection` (auditor other than the proposer) / `rejectCorrection`; original kept as `Superseded` with forward and backward links; revision counters of farm scope, lot and batch; farm verification reset; certificate becomes `Outdated`; result submissions carry the expected revision digest and the snapshot block number and hash, which must lie within the 256-block window; stale submissions and approvals are rejected | Reason and evidence documents | R7 (9), regression (5) | Capture of the auditing role |
| R8 | Confidentiality with integrity verification | Only identifiers, hashes and activity quantities on-chain; `matchesEvidence`; public certificate limited to total, phase shares and method fields | Evidence store; node-level permissioning of the Besu network | R8 (2) | On-chain quantities visible to members |
| R9 | Retention of off-chain evidence | `EvidenceRegistry.register` (persistent-ID hash, repository, retention, replicas), `extendRetention` (never shortened), `logRetrievalCheck` (auditors); submission and approval require the minimum retention and replication and no failed last retrieval check; a failed check blocks new results citing that evidence and is flagged on the certificate | Replicated repository with persistent identifiers | R9 (3), regression (3) | Repository loss or access failure |

Cross-cutting controls are role checks on all operational writes, separation of duties in factor approval, correction authorization and result approval, mandatory farm verification before result approval and idempotency against re-sent envelopes. The 107 tests comprise 55 requirement tests (R1–R9), 9 regression tests, 14 edge-case tests, 10 case-study tests, 15 tests of the methodological status and the mandatory mill inputs (`test/methodological-assurance.test.js`), 2 calculation-unit tests and 2 governance tests.
